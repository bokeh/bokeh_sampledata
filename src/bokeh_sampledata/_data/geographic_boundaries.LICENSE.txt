This notice accompanies geographic_boundaries.db and its JSON manifest.
Bokeh's software license is in the distribution's LICENSE.txt. The following
source-specific information applies to the boundary data.

WORLD COUNTRIES

Source: Natural Earth, Admin 0 countries, 1:50 million scale, version 5.1.1.
Authors: Natural Earth contributors, led by Tom Patterson and Nathaniel Vaughn
Kelso. Voluntary credit: Made with Natural Earth.

Dataset page:

Natural Earth declares all versions of its raster and vector map data public
domain. Its terms allow modification, redistribution, and commercial use;
permission and attribution are not required. This permits inclusion and
redistribution alongside Bokeh's BSD-3-Clause software without imposing
additional licensing obligations. The source data remains public domain;
its inclusion does not turn it into exclusively BSD-licensed data.

Terms verified October 18, 2026:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

These are de facto boundaries reflecting control, not necessarily legal claims.
They include countries and territories; identifiers may be missing, shared,
or unofficial. Natural Earth provides the data without assurances of accuracy
or fitness. See its boundary policy:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/disputed-boundaries-policy/

Bokeh retains selected source metadata and converts the shapes to compressed
2D WKB in SQLite. No simplification or reprojection is applied. No country
geometry required validity repair with the recorded generation toolchain.

US STATES AND COUNTIES

These are re-encodings of existing Bokeh sample data, not newly acquired
external datasets. Inputs: US_Regions_State_Boundaries.csv.gz and US_Counties.csv
from bokeh/bokeh_sampledata revision:
4195d5e5c3094ef578be168778c7e9501138f921
https://github.com/bokeh/bokeh_sampledata/tree/4195d5e5c3094ef578be168778c7e9501138f921/src/bokeh_sampledata/_data

That repository distributes the inputs under its Bokeh BSD-3-Clause license.
No separate upstream attribution, acquisition date, or source-specific license
was recorded with those files. This notice preserves that known provenance;
it does not assert an independently verified public-domain origin for them.
They are historical samples, not authoritative current administrative data.

Conversion preserves polygon interiors and repairs invalid polygon geometry,
retaining polygonal components and discarding collapsed line/point remnants.
Duplicate county FIPS identifiers retain the final source row. The JSON
manifest records original input hashes and every repaired identifier.

REPRODUCIBILITY

See tools/README.md in the source distribution for checksum-verified input
retrieval and deterministic regeneration using the committed Pixi lockfile.
The manifest records counts, input/output checksums and generation versions.
